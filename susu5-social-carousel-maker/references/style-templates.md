# Visual Style Templates

Use these as controlled visual style presets. Recommend only 3-5 relevant styles to the user.

## Recommended Bold Type Styles

Prioritize these styles when the user asks for bold color, bold typography, poster-like editorial impact, strong cover appeal, or less-detailed Xiaohongshu visuals.

### STYLE-BOLD-CURVED-TYPE

中文名: 弧形强字海报风

Best for: bold covers, AI/tool themes, personal growth hooks, career topics, attention-grabbing Xiaohongshu covers that need bold typography with a little softness.

Prompt style clause:

```text
Curved bold Swiss poster style, gigantic Chinese typography as the main visual, strict editorial grid, one restrained curved accent shape, high contrast palette, ultra-heavy Chinese display type, tiny condensed monospace English metadata, optional thin Songti-style Chinese micro note, minimal information, strong poster impact.
```

Rules:

- Use 1 huge Chinese word or a 2-4 character Chinese phrase as the main visual.
- Use at most 2 main colors plus black/off-white.
- Use one controlled curved shape: arc, semicircle, crescent, or wave panel.
- Keep the layout sparse; no sidebars, directories, diagrams, dense cards, or long body copy.
- Create font contrast: ultra-heavy Chinese display type + tiny mono English + optional thin serif Chinese micro note.
- Prefer collision palettes such as off-white + black + IKB blue, lemon yellow + black + warm white, acid green + black + gray, or orange-red + deep blue + off-white.
- Avoid liquid blobs, rainbow gradients, soft corporate gradients, many decorative shapes, and over-detailed infographic layouts.

### STYLE-BOLD-SWISS-POSTER

中文名: 强字瑞士海报风

Best for: high-impact covers, AI/product launches, strong claims, topic-openers, Swiss poster style, content that should look sharp and memorable instead of explanatory.

Prompt style clause:

```text
Bold Swiss poster style, oversized Chinese typography, strict Swiss grid, one high-saturation anchor color, pure rectangular color blocks, warm off-white paper background, black ink text, hairline rules, minimal metadata, no decoration, no information clutter.
```

Rules:

- Use one high-saturation anchor color only: IKB blue, lemon yellow, safety orange, acid green, or signal red.
- Use hard-edged rectangular color blocks and strong alignment.
- Let typography be the image; do not rely on icons or small illustrations.
- Keep text very short: one huge title plus one small metadata line.
- Avoid sidebars, icons, charts, detailed infographics, rounded UI cards, shadows, and gradients.

## Core Styles

### STYLE-KNOWLEDGE-CARD

Best for: dry knowledge, tutorials, frameworks, AI tools, career growth.

Prompt style clause:

```text
Clean Xiaohongshu knowledge-card style, light background, modular content blocks, large Chinese headline, one bright accent color, strong hierarchy, high mobile readability.
```

Caution: avoid making every page a boxy slide; use visual rhythm.

### STYLE-EDITORIAL-MAGAZINE

Best for: opinions, trends, brand stories, people, long-form article digestion.

Prompt style clause:

```text
Premium editorial magazine style, refined typography, strong whitespace, subtle paper texture, elegant composition, restrained color palette, thoughtful and polished.
```

Caution: keep in-image text short; the style loses quality when overloaded.

### STYLE-MINIMAL-PREMIUM

Best for: high-end services, consulting, personal brands, portfolios.

Prompt style clause:

```text
Minimal premium style, large whitespace, refined Chinese typography, muted palette, very few visual elements, quiet luxury feeling, clean and confident.
```

Caution: needs sharp copy; weak titles look empty.

### STYLE-BRAND-POSTER

Best for: product introduction, course selling, campaign promotion, service packaging.

Prompt style clause:

```text
Polished brand poster style, bold headline, strong visual anchor, limited palette, premium commercial layout, clear selling points, trustworthy and conversion-oriented.
```

Caution: avoid hard-sell clutter and exaggerated claims.

### STYLE-DATA-REPORT

Best for: industry analysis, data conclusions, B2B content, research digest.

Prompt style clause:

```text
Clean data report style, large numbers, structured grid, simple charts, high-trust business visual language, one accent color, crisp and analytical.
```

Caution: use only real or user-approved data.

### STYLE-HANDWRITTEN-NOTE

Best for: study notes, lifestyle, parenting, travel, personal experience.

Prompt style clause:

```text
Warm notebook style, soft background, hand-drawn markers, subtle sticker accents, personal and friendly, organized but not too formal, mobile-readable.
```

Caution: keep it tidy; do not let stickers become noise.

### STYLE-COMIC-EXPLAINER

Best for: science communication, difficult concepts, story-based education, emotional resonance.

Prompt style clause:

```text
Light comic explainer style, simple character scenes, friendly storytelling, clear visual labels, approachable and easy to understand, not childish or overly decorative.
```

Caution: do not use childish characters for serious B2B content.

### STYLE-PRODUCT-SEEDING

Best for: product recommendations, tools, courses, services, experience-based content.

Prompt style clause:

```text
Lifestyle product seeding style, realistic usage context, benefit cards, soft commercial photography feeling, approachable and trustworthy, not hard-sell.
```

Caution: do not invent product screenshots, logos, certifications, or results.

Recommendation rules:

- Bold type cover: recommend `STYLE-BOLD-CURVED-TYPE`, `STYLE-BOLD-SWISS-POSTER`, `STYLE-SWISS-GRID`, `STYLE-EDITORIAL-MAGAZINE`.
- Knowledge/tutorial: recommend `STYLE-BOLD-CURVED-TYPE` for bold covers, then `STYLE-KNOWLEDGE-CARD`, `STYLE-ACADEMIC-CLEAN`, `STYLE-SWISS-GRID`, `STYLE-MINDMAP-SYSTEM`.
- Product/service: recommend `STYLE-PRODUCT-SEEDING`, `STYLE-BRAND-POSTER`, `STYLE-MINIMAL-PREMIUM`, `STYLE-UI-SCREENSHOT-REDESIGN`.
- Report/data: recommend `STYLE-DATA-REPORT`, `STYLE-SWISS-GRID`, `STYLE-CLEAN-FINANCE`, `STYLE-NEWSPAPER-BRIEF`.
- Lifestyle/emotion: recommend `STYLE-SOFT-PASTEL`, `STYLE-HANDWRITTEN-NOTE`, `STYLE-WARM-HUMANIST`, `STYLE-FILM-DOCUMENTARY`.
- Brand/culture: recommend `STYLE-EDITORIAL-MAGAZINE`, `STYLE-RETRO-RISO`, `STYLE-YOUTH-STREET`, `STYLE-BOLD-POP`.
