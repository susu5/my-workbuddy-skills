# Carousel Structures

Choose a structure before writing page copy.

## STRUCT-KNOWLEDGE-6

Best for tutorials, explainers, AI tools, professional methods.

1. PAGE-COVER
2. PAGE-PROBLEM
3. PAGE-INSIGHT
4. PAGE-METHOD
5. PAGE-CHECKLIST
6. PAGE-SUMMARY

## STRUCT-PRODUCT-SEEDING-7

Best for products, services, courses, tools, offers.

1. PAGE-COVER
2. PAGE-PROBLEM
3. PAGE-PRODUCT
4. PAGE-CASE
5. PAGE-COMPARISON
6. PAGE-CHECKLIST
7. PAGE-SUMMARY

## STRUCT-ARTICLE-DIGEST-8

Best for turning long articles or reports into a carousel.

1. PAGE-COVER
2. PAGE-INSIGHT
3. PAGE-DATA
4. PAGE-METHOD
5. PAGE-CASE
6. PAGE-COMPARISON
7. PAGE-QUOTE
8. PAGE-SUMMARY

## STRUCT-STORY-7

Best for personal experience, brand story, founder story, event recap.

1. PAGE-COVER
2. PAGE-PROBLEM
3. PAGE-CASE
4. PAGE-INSIGHT
5. PAGE-METHOD
6. PAGE-QUOTE
7. PAGE-SUMMARY

## STRUCT-REPORT-9

Best for industry analysis, market research, performance recap.

1. PAGE-COVER
2. PAGE-PROBLEM
3. PAGE-DATA
4. PAGE-DATA
5. PAGE-INSIGHT
6. PAGE-COMPARISON
7. PAGE-METHOD
8. PAGE-CHECKLIST
9. PAGE-SUMMARY

Rules:

- Default to 6-9 pages.
- If the user asks for fewer pages, keep cover, 2-3 body pages, and summary.
- If the source article is dense, summarize first; do not force every paragraph into a page.
- Page count must be confirmed by the user during the content-plan gate.
