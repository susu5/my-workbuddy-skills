# Page Roles

Assign one role to every page before choosing image templates.

| ID | Role | Purpose | Required Content | Text Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PAGE-COVER | Cover hook | Stop the scroll and earn the click | strong headline, short subtitle, 1-3 keywords or benefit points | headline 8-20 Chinese chars when possible |
| PAGE-PROBLEM | Problem framing | Make the reader feel the pain or question | pain point, scene, consequence | 1 title + 2-3 short lines |
| PAGE-INSIGHT | Core insight | Deliver the main perspective | one idea, reason, implication | one core sentence + 2 bullets |
| PAGE-METHOD | Method/framework | Teach a reusable method | framework name, 3-5 parts | 3-5 compact items |
| PAGE-STEPS | Tutorial steps | Show how to do it | ordered steps | 3-5 steps |
| PAGE-CHECKLIST | Checklist | Make content saveable | checklist title, items | 4-7 short items |
| PAGE-COMPARISON | Comparison | Clarify before/after or wrong/right | two sides, contrast labels | 2-3 points per side |
| PAGE-DATA | Data proof | Add credibility | number, meaning, source note if available | one main number + 1-2 notes |
| PAGE-CASE | Case/story | Make the content concrete | scene, action, result | 3 short beats |
| PAGE-PRODUCT | Product/service benefit | Explain why the offer matters | pain point, benefit, proof | 3 benefits max |
| PAGE-QUOTE | Quote/opinion | Create shareable statement | quote, supporting line | quote dominates |
| PAGE-SUMMARY | Summary/CTA | Close and guide action | takeaway, save/comment/follow prompt | 1 takeaway + 1 CTA |

Rules:

- One page, one job.
- If a page needs two jobs, split it.
- Do not use `PAGE-DATA` without real or user-approved data.
- Do not make the cover a summary page; it must be a hook.
